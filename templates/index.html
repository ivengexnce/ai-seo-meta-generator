Why does Google truncate titles exceeding 60 characters or 600 pixels?

📐

Google allocates a fixed desktop container of 600px (around 960px on mobile) for SERP title tags.

When an HTML <title> exceeds this visual container, the search engine replaces the trailing characters with an ellipsis (...), amputating your call to action and brand authority. Rankora enforces a strict mathematical bound between 50 and 60 characters (weighted against variable font glyph kerning like 'W' vs 'i') so your SERP preview renders with 100% pixel fidelity.

Title Target: 50–60 Chars Viewport: ~600px Desktop Ellipsis Threshold: > 60 Chars

What are the exact requirements for Open Graph & Twitter Card social tags?

🖼️

Social previews require an optimal 1200 × 630px (1.91:1) dimension for retina clarity across LinkedIn, X, and Facebook.

Missing or malformed Open Graph tags cause social networks to scrape random page images or display broken thumbnail cards. Rankora exports production-validated og:title, og:description, og:image, og:url, twitter:card, and twitter:image tags complete with absolute HTTPS canonical URL resolution.

Dimensions: 1200 × 630 px Aspect Ratio: 1.91 : 1 Protocol: OGP v1.0 + Twitter v2

How do Google Search Console verification tokens integrate into deployments?

🛡️

Direct meta injection and dual automated verification endpoints ensure instant ownership verification.

Google Search Console verifies domain ownership via an HTML meta tag or an HTML file at the root. Rankora injects your token into the generated <head> code export and dynamically serves verification files at /google<token>.html, enabling instant verification on Vercel, Netlify, Render, or self-hosted servers.

Format: meta + file verification Verification: Zero DNS Delay
