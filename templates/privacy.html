1. Privacy-First Philosophy

Rankora was built from the ground up on a fundamental commitment: your content belongs to you. We believe you should be able to calibrate search metadata, test Google SERP snippets, and preview Open Graph tags without being tracked, profiled, or forced to create an account.

Key Guarantee: Rankora does not require user registration, does not deploy tracking pixels or behavioral cookies, and never monetizes or sells your data.

2. What We Do NOT Collect

To be completely transparent, we want to clarify what we never harvest:

  • No Content Harvesting: We do not log, catalog, or build datasets from the webpage content or marketing copy you paste into the generator console.
  • No Ad Trackers: We do not use Google AdSense, Meta Pixel, Hotjar, or any third-party behavioral analytics scripts.
  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for your phone number, billing address, or credit card information.

3. Real-Time AI Inference Processing

When you submit text to generate metadata via the /generate endpoint:

  • Your submitted text is transmitted via secure, encrypted TLS (HTTPS) directly to Google Gemini API servers.
  • The content is processed transiently in volatile server memory solely to synthesize titles, descriptions, and keyword suggestions.
  • Once the JSON response is delivered back to your browser, the text is immediately discarded from memory. It is never stored on disk or used to train public machine learning models.

4. Client-Side Browser Storage (`localStorage`)

Rankora operates with a Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) model and zero server-side user accounts. All stateful preferences are stored exclusively on your device inside your browser's localStorage:

  • gemini_api_key: Your optional Google AI Studio API key. It never touches Rankora backend databases and is transmitted directly in client request headers.
  • rankora_user_feedback: A local cache of feedback actions you have taken.

You can purge this data at any time directly through your browser's "Clear Site Data" settings.

5. Feedback & Voluntary Communications

If you choose to submit feedback or report a bug through our studio or feedback dialogs:

  • Your message, optional category, and any handle you provide are logged to our internal feedback repository so that our development team can review suggestions and address technical issues.
  • If you voluntarily provide an email address, it is used strictly to reply to your inquiry and is never shared with third parties or used for promotional spam.
  • Personal feedback logs are strictly access-controlled and never publicly broadcast.

6. Third-Party Sub-Processors

Rankora relies on reputable, enterprise-grade infrastructure providers:

  • Google Cloud & Google Gemini API: AI inference processing (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Vercel & Render: Edge application delivery, serverless hosting, and DNS management.

7. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

In accordance with the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you retain full rights over your data:

  • Right to Deletion: Because all personal settings reside in your browser's local storage, clearing your browser cache immediately deletes your local profile.
  • Right to Inquire: You can contact the maintainer at any time to request the inspection or deletion of any feedback you voluntarily submitted with your email address.
  • No Sale of Personal Data: We do not sell personal data under CCPA or any international data privacy regime.
