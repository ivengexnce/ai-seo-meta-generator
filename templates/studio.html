Privacy Policy

Rankora is privacy-first by design.

  • Zero Tracking / Zero Telemetry: We do not log, harvest, or sell the text content you paste into the generator.
  • Local Browser Storage: Custom API keys and UI preferences are stored strictly in your browser's localStorage and never sent to our servers.
  • Direct Model Inference: Requests are processed either through your configured Google Gemini API endpoint or securely relayed with zero persistent storage.
  • Google Search Console Ownership: Verification tokens are strictly utilized for standard HTTP verification handshakes.
